BeamSwap, a leading **DEX and DeFi hub on the Moonbeam parachain**, was launched as a **community-driven** alternative to the often venture capital-dominated DeFi landscape. The platform offers a comprehensive suite of decentralized financial services, including an **Automated Market Maker (AMM)**, **yield farming**, **staking and liquid staking**, and a **launchpad** for new projects.

BeamSwap is a **strong proponent of staking**, even enabling instant withdrawals with the xcDOT solution. In addition to its core AMM functionality, BeamSwap provides **cross-chain bridges** to other blockchain ecosystems, enabling seamless asset transfer and interaction with the broader Web3 DeFi landscape.

## Key Features
1. **Automated Market Maker (AMM)**: BeamSwap utilizes a traditional AMM model for swapping tokens, where liquidity is provided by users earning fees in return. This model is the foundation of the DEX's service.
2. **Yield Farming**: Users can stake their LP (liquidity provider) tokens in yield farms to earn additional rewards, typically in the platform's native token, GLINT, enhancing their returns on provided liquidity.
3. **Staking**: The platform provides a user-friendly staking interface for token holders to stake their GLINT tokens to earn a share of the protocol's trading fees.
4. **Liquid Staking**: With xcDOT, BeamSwap offers liquid staking, enabling users to stake DOT while maintaining liquidity. This allows users to participate in DeFi and staking simultaneously.
5. **Cross-chain Swaps**: The platform integrates bridges, enabling users to swap assets across different blockchains via one interface.
6. **Launchpad**: A platform for new projects to launch their tokens and raise funds within the BeamSwap ecosystem.

## Challenges and Transition to BeamSwap V3
Like many DeFi protocols, BeamSwap faced the challenge of balancing the interests of early adopters and long-term token holders. The team recognized that the tokenomics required adjustment to ensure a more equitable distribution of rewards and to support the protocol's health.

The introduction of **BeamSwap V3** marked a significant upgrade, transitioning from a reward structure based on a "revenue-generating AMM with inflationary emission rewards" to a **fee-generating AMM with deflationary emission rewards**. This shift aimed to create a more sustainable model by reducing inflation and aligning the protocol's design with the long-term success of the GLINT token.

The V3 upgrade included:
- **Reduction of GLINT emission rewards** to adjust token supply.
- **Introduction of a Buyback and Burn Mechanism** where a percentage of protocol revenue is used to buy back GLINT tokens and remove them from circulation.
- **Implementation of a protocol fee** to fund the buyback and burn process and to contribute to the DAO treasury.

## The BeamSwap Ecosystem
BeamSwap is more than a simple DEX. It represents a full-fledged DeFi ecosystem built on the Moonbeam parachain, taking advantage of the **interoperability and security** offered by the Polkadot network. The platform's evolution to V3 demonstrates its commitment to long-term sustainability and community governance.

### Key Information
- **Chain:** Moonbeam
- **Primary Token:** GLINT
- **Key Offerings:** AMM, Yield Farming, Staking, Liquid Staking (xcDOT), Launchpad, Cross-chain Swaps
- **V3 Features:** Buyback and Burn, Protocol Fee, Deflationary Tokenomics

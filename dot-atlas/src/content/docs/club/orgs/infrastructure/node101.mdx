node101 provides paid Polkadot RPC services and managed dedicated nodes for developers and infrastructure teams. Its service combines node operations, distributed request handling, monitoring, and round-the-clock technical support, helping teams focus on their applications.

## Polkadot RPC services

- **Flexible access:** JSON-RPC support, with HTTP and WebSocket access available according to the selected plan.
- **Operational visibility:** Dashboard options for monitoring usage, endpoints, performance, and service status.
- **Managed operations:** Distributed infrastructure, 24/7 technical support, and incident reporting options.
- **Additional services:** Archive nodes and testnet RPC support can be requested separately.

## Plans and dedicated infrastructure

Published Polkadot plans range from 250 to 2,000 compute units per second, with monthly allowances from 648 million requests to unlimited requests. The service page lists average response times of 200–250 ms depending on the plan. These figures describe the published plans and are not a guarantee for every request.

Dedicated infrastructure can be scoped across Türkiye, Europe, and the United States according to deployment requirements and availability. node101 offers paid services; it does not offer a free public RPC endpoint.

Explore [Polkadot plans and service details](https://node101.io/en/rpc/polkadot), or contact [hello@node101.io](mailto:hello@node101.io) to discuss a deployment.
